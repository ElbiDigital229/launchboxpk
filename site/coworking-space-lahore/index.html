In plain words

What a coworking space in Lahore actually is

A coworking space is a shared professional workspace where freelancers, remote workers, startups and small teams rent flexible desks or private cabins instead of signing a traditional office lease. Across DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town and Model Town, plans run from PKR 1,500 a day for a day pass to PKR 22,500 a month for a dedicated desk, with private cabins from PKR 20,000 per person a month.

DHA Phase 5 is the most popular choice for tech freelancers and founders: it’s secure, the infrastructure is reliable, and restaurants and banks are close. Launchbox removes the overhead of deposits, furniture, utilities and long commitments, and gives you a professional place built for focused work.

Questions

Quick answers

What is the best coworking space in Lahore?

Launchbox is a coworking space in DHA Phase 5, Lahore, built for freelancers, remote workers and startup teams. It offers flexible desks, dedicated desks and private cabins with 150 Mbps fibre internet, backup power, meeting rooms and no lock-in contracts.

How much does coworking cost in Lahore?

Between PKR 1,500 a day and PKR 22,500 a month depending on the type of workspace. At Launchbox: day passes PKR 1,500 a day, flexible desks PKR 15,000 a month, dedicated desks PKR 22,500 a month, private cabins PKR 20,000 per person a month (minimum 4).

Is coworking cheaper than renting an office?

Yes. A small office in DHA Lahore costs PKR 50,000 to 100,000+ a month all-in. Launchbox starts at PKR 1,500 a day with everything included.

Can I work from a café instead?

You can, but cafés lack reliable internet, backup power, meeting rooms and quiet. You’ll also spend PKR 500 to 1,500 a day on food just to justify your seat.

Where exactly is Launchbox?

First Floor, 38-A CCA, Sector C, DHA Phase 5, Lahore. Easy to reach from Gulberg, Johar Town and Model Town.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month. No deposits, no lock-in, no hidden fees.

Flexible desk or dedicated desk?

Flexible (PKR 15,000 a month) means any available desk in the shared area. Dedicated (PKR 22,500 a month) is your own reserved desk, same spot every day, with storage.

Is the internet reliable for video calls?

Yes. Dedicated high-speed fibre, not shared residential broadband. Built for video calls and uploads, and backup power keeps you online during load-shedding.

Can I visit before signing up?

Yes. Book a free tour or message us on WhatsApp.
