Coworking basics

What is a coworking space?

A coworking space is a shared professional workspace where freelancers, remote workers, startups, and small teams work independently while sharing infrastructure like internet, desks, meeting rooms, and common areas. It offers the benefits of a full office without the cost or commitment of a traditional lease.

How is coworking different from a cafe?

Coworking spaces offer dedicated desks, reliable high-speed internet, backup power, meeting rooms, and a professional environment. Cafes have none of these guarantees. Coworking also gives you a consistent routine and a community of professionals — something a cafe cannot provide.

Who uses coworking spaces?

Freelancers, remote workers, startup founders, small agencies, consultants, and anyone who needs a professional workspace without renting a full office. At Launchbox, most members are freelancers and early-stage startup teams based in Lahore.

Is coworking better than working from home?

It depends on what is failing at home. If your internet drops during client calls, if load-shedding kills your working day, or if you struggle to separate work from home life, a coworking space solves all three. If you already have reliable power, fast internet, and a quiet room, working from home is cheaper. Most Launchbox members join because their home setup could not survive a client call.

Is coworking cheaper than renting an office?

Almost always, for small teams. A private office requires a security deposit, a lease, furniture, internet installation, a UPS or generator, and someone to manage it all. A coworking membership bundles every one of those into one monthly figure with no deposit and no lease. Renting your own office generally makes financial sense only once a team is large enough to fill it full-time.

Do I need to be a freelancer to join Launchbox?

No. Launchbox is built around freelancers, remote workers, and startup teams, but anyone who needs a professional place to work is welcome — consultants, visiting professionals, and people preparing for interviews or certifications all use the space.

Pricing & plans

How much does coworking cost at Launchbox?

Day Pass: PKR 1,500/day. Flexible Desk: PKR 15,000/month. Dedicated Desk: PKR 22,500/month. Private Cabin: PKR 20,000/person/month (minimum 4 persons). All plans include internet, power, tea and coffee, and common area access. No hidden fees.

What's included in the price?

Every plan includes 150 Mbps fibre internet, backup power, tea and coffee, kitchen and common area access, and printing access. Dedicated desks add storage and meeting room hours. Private cabins add 24/7 access and unlimited meeting room use. There is nothing extra to buy to work normally.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No. All plans are month-to-month. You can start, pause, or cancel anytime with no penalties or long-term commitments.

Do you take a security deposit?

No. Launchbox does not take a security deposit on any plan. You pay for the month you are using and nothing up front.

What is the difference between a flexible desk and a dedicated desk?

A flexible desk (PKR 15,000/month) is open seating — you take any free desk on the day you come in. A dedicated desk (PKR 22,500/month) is a specific desk that is always yours, so you can leave a monitor or equipment set up, and it includes storage and meeting room hours. Choose flexible if you come in a few days a week, dedicated if the space is your main office.

What is a private cabin and who is it for?

A private cabin is an enclosed room for a team, priced at PKR 20,000 per person per month with a minimum of 4 people. It includes 24/7 access and unlimited meeting room use. Cabins suit agencies and funded startups who need to talk freely without disturbing the shared floor.

Can I upgrade or switch plans?

Yes. You can move from a flexible desk to a dedicated desk or private cabin at any time, subject to availability. Your plan adjusts from the next billing cycle.

What payment methods do you accept?

Launchbox accepts bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and cash payments.

When is payment due?

Payment is due at the start of each billing month. Members are sent a reminder before their due date.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. The price on the card is the price you pay — internet, power, tea and coffee, common areas and printing are all included. There is no deposit, no joining fee, and no exit fee.

Do you offer a free trial or a free visit?

Internet, power & infrastructure

How fast is the internet at Launchbox?

The space runs on 150 Mbps fibre. It is provisioned for the whole floor to work simultaneously, including video calls, large uploads, and screen sharing.

Do you have backup internet?

Yes. Launchbox runs a backup ISP alongside the primary fibre connection, so a single provider outage does not stop the space working.

What happens during load-shedding?

Nothing changes for you. Launchbox runs UPS plus a generator, so power to desks and internet continues through LESCO outages. This is the single most common reason members join — a dropped client call costs more than a month of coworking.

Do you have a UPS and a generator?

Both. UPS covers the instant of a cut so nothing reboots, and the generator carries longer outages.

Can I take Zoom or Google Meet calls?

Yes. The space is quiet and professional enough for client calls, the bandwidth holds up for video, and meeting rooms are available when you need privacy or a neutral background.

Is the internet fast enough for large file uploads?

Yes. 150 Mbps fibre handles large design files, video, and code pushes. Members who deliver heavy files to international clients are a core part of who the space is built for.

Will my work be interrupted by outages?

Power and internet are both backed up — UPS and generator for electricity, a secondary ISP for connectivity. The point of the space is that outages become invisible to you.
