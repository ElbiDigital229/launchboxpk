Why we built it

We started Launchbox because we couldn’t find what we wanted as freelancers and remote workers in Lahore. Cafés had unreliable Wi-Fi and a forced food bill. Home offices killed momentum every time the lights went out. The available coworking spaces in the city felt either overpriced for funded startups or stitched together as an afterthought.

We wanted a workspace that respected your time. Fast internet that holds up at 2 PM the same as 9 AM. Power that doesn’t blink when LESCO does. Meeting rooms you can actually book. A community of people who are heads-down on real work, not networking events. And pricing that didn’t require a funding round to afford.

So we built it. Launchbox opened in DHA Phase 5 with a simple promise: a professional space, run honestly, priced fairly. Day passes for people testing the waters, monthly desks for full-time freelancers, private cabins for early-stage teams. No deposits. No 12-month contracts. No upsells.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What are your working hours?

Launchbox is open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM, and closed on Sundays. Members with private cabins can arrange extended or 24/7 access depending on their plan.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No. All plans are month-to-month. You can start, pause, or cancel anytime with no penalties or long-term commitments.

Do you take a security deposit?

No. Launchbox does not take a security deposit on any plan. You pay for the month you are using and nothing up front.

How fast is the internet at Launchbox?

The space runs on 150 Mbps fibre. It is provisioned for the whole floor to work simultaneously, including video calls, large uploads, and screen sharing.

What happens during load-shedding?

Nothing changes for you. Launchbox runs UPS plus a generator, so power to desks and internet continues through LESCO outages. This is the single most common reason members join — a dropped client call costs more than a month of coworking.

Do you offer a free trial or a free visit?

You can visit the space for free during business hours and see it before committing — no sales pitch. Book a visit through the website or message us on WhatsApp.

What payment methods do you accept?

Launchbox accepts bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and cash payments.
