Day Pass questions

Before you start

How much is a day pass?

PKR 1,500 for a full day, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM.

What’s included?

A shared desk for the day, high-speed internet with backup, backup power, tea and coffee, the kitchen and common areas, and printing.

How do I pay?

Launchbox accepts cash, bank transfer, JazzCash and EasyPaisa.

No. You pay for the day you use, and that’s it.

When does a flexible desk make more sense?

If you come in more than about ten days a month, a flexible desk at PKR 15,000 a month works out cheaper.
