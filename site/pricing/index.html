Pricing questions

Before you sign up

How much does coworking cost at Launchbox?

Day Pass is PKR 1,500 a day, Flexible Desk is PKR 15,000 a month, Dedicated Desk is PKR 22,500 a month, and Private Cabin is PKR 20,000 per person a month (minimum 4 people). All plans include high-speed internet, backup power, tea and coffee, and access to common areas.

Is there a contract or lock-in period?

No. All plans are month-to-month. You can start, pause or cancel anytime with no penalties or long-term commitments.

Can I upgrade or switch plans later?

Yes. You can move from a flexible desk to a dedicated desk or private cabin at any time, subject to availability. We adjust your plan from the next billing cycle.

What is included in all plans?

Every plan includes high-speed internet, backup power, tea and coffee, access to common areas and the kitchen, printing access, and a professional work environment.

Can I try the space before committing?

Yes. Book a free tour to see the space, try the setup and meet the community before choosing a plan.
